Does a quote request guarantee a job?

No. A request is an opportunity to price work, not a guaranteed booking or revenue.

Do I need to make the video myself?

No. You take a before and an after photo. The finished video, with your name, number and registration on it, is ready to share before you're back in the van.

Does this replace my waste compliance responsibilities?

It does most of the work: reads your tip receipt, fills in the transfer note and keeps every job's record ready for the new waste-tracking rules. You still check and sign it.
