# Publishing to CleanUpPic: carrier terms

*First draft, in plain English. To be reviewed before it is relied on.*

These terms cover publishing your job videos to CleanUpPic from inside your own app. By ticking to accept, the business owner agrees to them for the business.

## Who can publish
- You must be a registered upper tier waste carrier, and keep your registration current.
- Your details must be true: your business name, your email and your WhatsApp number.

## What you publish
- You choose every video. Nothing goes on CleanUpPic unless you tap Publish on that video.
- Every video must be your own photos of your own real job.
- No house numbers, number plates, people's faces or private papers may show.

## Your videos
- You keep ownership of your photos and videos. You give us permission to store them, show them on CleanUpPic and promote them.
- You can remove a video from CleanUpPic at any time from your app. Turning publishing off removes all of them.
- We can remove a video or stop your publishing if it breaks these terms.

## Quote requests
- Quote requests from your videos go to the email address you give us. You deal with the customer yourself.

## Fly-tipping
- You never fly-tip. If fly-tipping by you or your business is proven, we end your place on CleanUpPic.

## Changes
- If these terms change, you will be asked to accept the new version in your app before you publish again.
